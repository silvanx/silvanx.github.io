I am a computational neuroscientist specialising in closed-loop neuromodulation, deep brain stimulation, and control-based approaches to brain dynamics. My work combines mechanistic modelling, simulation, and algorithm design to study how stimulation interacts with neural tissue and to develop robust adaptive control strategies.

I have led research projects in computational neuroscience, developed finite-element and conductance-based models, and built reproducible simulation workflows in Python and NEURON. I have also taught postgraduate courses, supervised students, and secured over €200,000 in competitive research funding.

I am available for consulting, project-based technical support, tutoring, and methods review in DBS, computational neuroscience, neural modelling, scientific computing, and related neurotechnology projects.
